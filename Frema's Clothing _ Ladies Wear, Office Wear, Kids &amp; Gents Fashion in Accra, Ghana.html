FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions Customers Ask About Frema's Clothing

What does Frema's Clothing sell?

Frema's Clothing sells Ladies Wear, Office Wear, Kids clothes, shoes and bags, and Gents fashion in Accra, Ghana. The boutique offers dresses, office sets, everyday outfits, shirts and children's pieces in selected sizes and colours. Each product page shows the price in Ghana cedis, available sizes, colours and stock, so shoppers can choose with confidence before ordering online.

How do I order from Frema's Clothing?

To order from Frema's Clothing, browse the shop, choose a product, then select a size and colour and add it to your cart. Open the cart, review your items, and continue to checkout. Enter your full name, phone number and delivery location, then place your order. The boutique will confirm delivery and payment details with you on WhatsApp before dispatch.

Does Frema's Clothing deliver in Accra and across Ghana?

Yes. Frema's Clothing delivers in Accra and across Ghana. At checkout, enter your town or area and a landmark so the boutique can arrange delivery. The delivery fee is confirmed on WhatsApp before dispatch, and the final amount shown at checkout excludes that fee. Delivery details, including your location and any instructions, are included with your order.

What payment methods does Frema's Clothing accept?

Payment is not yet configured at Frema's Clothing. Checkout creates a Pending Payment order, and the boutique confirms payment and delivery details on WhatsApp before dispatch. The store is preparing support for Mobile Money and card payments through Paystack or Hubtel. No card payment is taken or simulated on this website, and no payment is collected until the boutique confirms.

What sizes are available?

Sizes vary by product and are shown on each product page. Ladies Wear and Office Wear use XS to XL, while Gents pieces use M to XL. Kids clothes use ages 2–3 through 10–12. Select a size before adding an item to your cart. If you are unsure about fit, contact the boutique on WhatsApp before ordering.

How do I contact Frema's Clothing on WhatsApp?

You can contact Frema's Clothing on WhatsApp using the WhatsApp buttons on the storefront, checkout confirmation page, and order pages. The boutique's WhatsApp number is shown in Settings and is used to confirm delivery, payment and order details. For enquiries, use the WhatsApp us link in the footer. Include your order reference, if you have one.
